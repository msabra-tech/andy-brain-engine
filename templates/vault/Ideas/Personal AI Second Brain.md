# Personal AI Second Brain

## Core Idea

Turn messy captures into a clear personal knowledge map that stays useful on a phone. Source: [[References/Sources/Initial Setup|Initial Setup]].

## Useful Behaviors

- Pull out tasks and reminders.
- Keep ideas separate from commitments.
- Preserve source links.
- Show uncertainty instead of hiding it.
- Avoid duplicates when the same thing is mentioned twice.
